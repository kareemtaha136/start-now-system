Questions, answered

Before you start.

What is ZeroHours?

An anti-procrastination and academic execution system that helps students identify what is blocking them, choose a smaller next action, and begin.

Who is ZeroHours for?

Students who know what they need to do but regularly feel stuck, overwhelmed, distracted, or behind.

Is ZeroHours a planner?

Not simply. It includes academic organization, but connects that workload directly to tools for starting and continuing the work.

How does it help with procrastination?

It guides you from the reason you are avoiding a task to one useful next action, a five-minute start, and focused continuation.

Is it a subscription?

No. ZeroHours is a one-time purchase — $19.99, down from $29.99.

How do I get access after buying?

Open app.zerohours.store and enter the email you used at checkout. You only do it once per device.

Can I use it on my phone and laptop?

Yes, on up to 3 devices with the same purchase email.

I got a new phone / it's asking me to unlock again

Just enter your purchase email again. If it says you've hit the device limit, send us a message on the support page and we'll reset it.

Is my data private?

ZeroHours uses local-first data storage, keeping your working data on your device. Import and export tools help you manage your own copy.

Do I need an account?

No. You unlock ZeroHours with the email you used at checkout — no account or password.

Can I use it for different classes?

Yes. Courses, assignments, exams, notes, reading, schedules, and grades can be organized across classes.

What if I'm already behind?

Catch-Up Mode, Deadline Rescue, and Exam Rescue are designed to help you regain clarity and choose what to do next.

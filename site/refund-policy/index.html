ZeroHours offers a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of the original transaction date.

How to request a refund

  1. Visit paddle.net.
  2. Use the email address connected to your purchase.
  3. Select the ZeroHours order and request a refund, or contact Paddle’s purchase support.

Paddle is our Merchant of Record and handles payment support and returns. Providing your order email or transaction reference helps Paddle locate the purchase.

Processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. The time for funds to appear depends on the payment method and financial institution. Paddle will provide the applicable status and confirmation.

After a full refund, your license to use ZeroHours ends and you should stop using the app. Data you created locally remains under your control.

If you see a duplicate charge, cannot unlock the app with your purchase email, or experience another order problem, contact Paddle through paddle.net. Access problems can often be corrected without affecting your right to request a refund within the 14-day period.

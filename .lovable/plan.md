# Enrich the ZeroHours opening image

## Direction
Use the selected **Ambient Environmental Depth** treatment. Keep the current student, laptop angle, desk, and visible ZeroHours home screen, but make the room feel more lived-in and visually engaging.

## Changes
- Refine both the desktop and phone versions of the opening image.
- Add restrained study-space details around the subject: a softly blurred plant, a small stack of course books, and one warm practical light or subtle shelf element.
- Introduce natural window light, gentle shadows, and foreground-to-background depth.
- Keep the palette warm and neutral, with only subtle indigo/coral accents that connect to ZeroHours.
- Preserve a clean corridor around the laptop so the real app screen remains the sharpest, most readable focal point.
- Keep all added detail inside the photograph; do not add promotional text, labels, decorative UI, or animations.

## Quality checks
- Confirm the laptop screen is unchanged and readable.
- Confirm the student and desk remain natural rather than staged or cluttered.
- Check the existing desktop 8:5 and phone 4:5 placements without cropping important content.
- Test the opening section at phone, tablet, and desktop widths for overflow or layout regressions.

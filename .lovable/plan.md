# Add ZeroHours legal policy pages

## What will be added
- A public Privacy Notice identifying Cameron Taha as the data controller, explaining data collected, purposes, legal bases, sharing, retention, security, cookies, international transfers, and user rights.
- Public Terms & Conditions covering product use, acceptable-use rules, intellectual property, digital delivery, Paddle payment terms and Merchant of Record disclosure, service availability, and suspension or termination.
- A public Refund Policy with Paddle’s minimum compliant 14-day refund period and clear instructions to request a refund through Paddle.
- Links to all three policies in the main website footer, with mobile-friendly layouts matching the existing ZeroHours design.

## Important policy decision
A “no refunds” clause cannot be used because Paddle requires a refund window between 14 and 90 days. The site will use the minimum 14-day period so it can pass payment-provider review.

## Technical details
- Create `/privacy`, `/terms`, and `/refund-policy` as separate public pages.
- Give each page unique search and social-sharing metadata.
- Reuse shared legal-page navigation and footer elements to keep the policies consistent and easy to maintain.
- Add the work to the project roadmap and validate each page and link on mobile and desktop.
